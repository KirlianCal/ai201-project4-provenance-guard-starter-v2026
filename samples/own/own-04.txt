To me, accountability means taking ownership of your responsibilities and following through, while also being honest if something isn’t going as planned. Even if there were heavy repercussions it is more important to communicate the mistake and work together to fix it. Teamwork is at its best when everyone takes responsibility not just for their own work, but for the overall outcome. I think it is also important to communicate when there is a hindrance, whether it is me or another teammate, so expectations are what they should be with no surprises.
