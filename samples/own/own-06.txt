Months of work were being loaded into the battered school van all at once. The frigid February morning didn’t help; everyone was hastily tossing their tools inside, eager to leave as quickly as possible. I couldn’t blame them—I was just as tired and groggy. My only hope was that the thin piece of cardboard would protect the bulletin board and its components from any damage. Neatness was crucial since we would be judged on it. When a wrench clattered out of someone's bag onto the pavement, I felt a pang in my chest. It just needed to survive the two-hour drive to the AgriPlex. Finally, after the last person added their bag to the pile, we were ready to go.
