They went through the awards alphabetically, each name announced with mounting suspense. When they reached the "P"s, my heart skipped a beat. It was almost our time. I tried to calm myself and reassured myself we’d placed—there was no way we hadn’t. The room went quiet, and I heard the loudspeaker announce “Promotional.” I held my breath. They announced third and second place, and I began to wonder—had we won the whole competition? But when the names of the winners were called, I was speechless. Our team wasn’t even in the top three.
