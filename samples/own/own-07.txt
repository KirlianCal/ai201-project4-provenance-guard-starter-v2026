The drive was mostly smooth, save for a few hiccups—bathroom breaks and traffic caused us to arrive slightly behind schedule. After waiting for everyone else to unload their belongings, my teammates and I decided to see where the other teams had set up their boards. As we entered the building, we were met with a sea of people, all dressed the same in striking red blazers and black office slacks. It almost felt like a cult meeting—everyone wearing the same thing and gathered for the same purpose. We navigated through the crowd and up a large staircase, making our way to a balcony overlooking the stairs. There was no way we’d be able to carry our board up those steps. Looking around, we saw our competition room just behind us. We had to find another way in. After a bit of maneuvering, we found a side entrance wide enough to accommodate our project. The door led to a long driveway with hotel housing on one side. We turned the corner of the building and found ourselves behind the van. Knowing exactly where we had to go, we grabbed the board and its bag of attachable pieces.
