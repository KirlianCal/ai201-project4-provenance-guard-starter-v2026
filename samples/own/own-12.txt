I couldn’t understand it. I had been so sure we’d win. I looked over at my teammate, the senior from last year, and saw tears welling in her eyes. I almost wanted to cry too. After all the long afternoons we spent working on this project, we didn’t even make the top five. In that moment, it felt like all our effort had been for nothing, like no matter how hard we tried, it wouldn’t matter. It felt like we had been robbed of something. It took us a while to accept it. We had lost the chance to compete in Georgia, all expenses paid. But even though it hurt, I look back now and remember the lessons we learned. Maybe next time it won’t hurt as badly.
